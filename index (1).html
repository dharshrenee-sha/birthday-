Happy Birthday, da! 🥹❤️

I'll be there to cheer for you when life feels beautiful, to encourage you when things get difficult, to protect you whenever I can, and to remind you of your worth when you forget it yourself.

And one little thing I want you to promise me… 🥺

I might not always be physically beside you, but my love, my prayers, and my support will always be with you. You can come to me with your happiness, your sadness, your failures, your fears, or even when you have absolutely nothing to say. And whatever happens, I hope you never forget how loved you are — not only on your happiest days, but especially on the days when you struggle to see your own light.

I'll always be rooting for you. In every phase. In every chapter. In every version of you.

Life may change a thousand things, but it will never change the fact that I'll always be here for you.

Love you so much, da. Always and forever. 🫂❤️
